# Keep incident lifecycle transitions human-controlled

An Incident progresses through `open`, `acknowledged`, `mitigated`, and `resolved`, with a resolved Incident able to be reopened. Automation may create an open Incident and assign or page its owner, but only a human may acknowledge, mitigate, resolve, or reopen it; every transition is preserved as an immutable Timeline Event even though the portfolio demo will focus on triage rather than showing the full response cycle.
