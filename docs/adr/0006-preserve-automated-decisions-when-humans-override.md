# Preserve automated decisions when humans override

Operational Judgments and Policy Decisions will remain immutable after they are recorded. A human may supersede an automated outcome only by appending a Human Override with the replacement outcome, actor, timestamp, and reason, allowing the system to reconstruct history and compare the original automation with the eventually accepted outcome. Operators see and act on one Current Priority; a superseded Priority Assessment remains visible only as historical audit evidence rather than as a competing active value.
